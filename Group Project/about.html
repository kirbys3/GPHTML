About Us

Welcome to Surfside Grill & Pizzeria, where every dish is a wave of flavor! Surfside is a laid-back, beach-themed eatery nestled in Jensen Beach on Hutchinson Island. Surfside has become a local favorite for residents and visitors looking for fresh flavors and friendly service. We serve and deliver Italian & American comfort food as well as offer hearty daily specials. Come in and see why we are your island spot for good food and great vibes!

Serving Jensen Beach since 1996

From the grill & the oven

Choose from hand-stretched pizzas, classic Italian favorites, fresh salads, sandwiches, seafood, and daily specials. Come by for lunch, stay for dinner, or order takeout and delivery.
